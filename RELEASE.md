## Safari Icon Fixer 0.6.3 — Icon Protection

- Applied custom artwork and display style are protected locally, independently of Safari.
- Scan to detect differences and prepare recovery with **Restore custom icon** or Quick Repair; review and apply only when Safari is closed.
- New full backups include the corresponding personalization state. Legacy backups restore the cache while retaining the current protected collection.
- Existing digest-only records are migrated when the original matching artwork is still present. Previously lost images cannot always be recovered.

The app is ad hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. Safari’s cache is undocumented; changes detected in the cache do not prove how Safari renders Favorites.
